02 Funding eligibility and accountability

Built so the program stays fundable

Most programs do not lose funding because the work did not happen. They lose it because the work could not be evidenced. Eligibility rules, intake, verification, and reporting are designed into the application from the first phase rather than reconstructed at renewal.

COVI & ELUV · participation layer

Participation that grows with the ecosystem.

COVI and ELUV are ConstructFi's participation layer—not the reason the ecosystem exists. ConstructFi leads with useful products and real-world value. COVI and ELUV are designed to recognize eligible participation, support defined access and member experiences, and connect people more deeply to the ecosystem as it grows.

COVI
Ecosystem utility layer

Built for participation across the construction ecosystem.

COVI is designed to recognize eligible activity across Covington, ConstructFi, construction commerce, learning, games, and participating products. It may support access to defined features, experiences, promotions, and member benefits as programs become available.

ERC-20 · Built on Base, settled to Ethereum · fixed cap 10,000,000,000

"My meaningful activity in the construction ecosystem can be recognized and connected."

ELUV
Recognition layer

Built to recognize progress in real estate.

ELUV is designed to recognize meaningful participation in the Eluvial ecosystem, including learning, skill development, engagement with real estate tools, community participation, and eligible programs. It may support access to defined educational experiences, tools, member opportunities, and community benefits as the platform evolves.

“My learning and growth in real estate can unlock deeper participation.”

About Us

ConstructFi is the connected ecosystem for the built world.

ConstructFi brings together marketplace commerce, specialized software, real estate intelligence, procurement, education, interactive learning, and custom digital solutions that help people and organizations build, operate, invest, learn, and grow.

ConstructFi is not simply an app, a marketplace, or a token project. It is the umbrella ecosystem that connects specialized products, communities, and opportunities for contractors, suppliers, developers, property owners, investors, entrepreneurs, nonprofits, public agencies, institutions, and future builders.
